\begin{abstract}
We give a computer-assisted proof candidate for the maximum forward Kullback--Leibler divergence from a distribution on three binary variables to a mixture of two Bernoulli products, equivalently the closure of RBM(3,1). The exact value is $-\tfrac34\log(2\sqrt3-3)$ nats; the only maximizing targets are the two uniform parity distributions. A three-multiplier log-supermodular certificate proves the lower bound. Reverse local stochastic channels reduce all targets to six support orbits, whose continuous simplices are covered by 16,600 exact witness pieces. A standard-library checker verifies 93,792 vertex conditions. We provide an executable rational target-to-witness interface and a conditioning corollary for mixtures of $2^{n-2}$ products on $n$ bits. The constant was conjectured previously. Producer replay and internal model review are not unaffiliated verification or journal peer review.
\end{abstract}
\textbf{Keywords:} Kullback--Leibler divergence; latent class model; Bernoulli mixture; restricted Boltzmann machine; exact certificate.
